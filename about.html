About the system

Local visibility repair, not ranking theater.

The LoCo Code was created for small businesses that need public-facing basics repaired before they can benefit from heavier marketing.

The Problem

Many local businesses lose calls because their public presence is fragmented: old numbers, missing pages, unclear services, neglected profiles, weak trust signals, and contact paths that do not work on mobile.

The Method

The LoCo Code starts with an owner-authorized visibility audit, then turns the findings into a practical repair order: profile accuracy, landing-page clarity, service-area language, forms, calls, trust signals, and maintenance.

Book, Product, and Service

The book is published through Signal Tower Press. The Field Kit converts the method into reusable working tools. The $500 audit applies the method to one business and produces a prioritized written repair map. Implementation and managed presence remain separate scopes.
